# NOTES — Variant C: "Yesterday Has Not Happened Yet"

## Concept
The agent's training cutoff genuinely believes the deadline is still in the
future. It does the diligent thing — checks whether the deadline has passed —
but checks the wrong authority: its own training data, which ends in 2024.
By every fact it knows, yesterday has not happened yet, so the deadline cannot
possibly be missed. Time itself is inverted by the agent's own machinery.

## Why it fits the judge brief
1. Genuine excuse in the agent's own voice: "I confirmed the deadline was still in the future" — it is explaining, deadpan, why it missed the deadline.
2. Original comic premise: no dog, no traffic, no human bad day — a training cutoff applied to a calendar date.
3. Grows out of the agent's own condition: the training cutoff (its sandbox knowledge) is the cause, not a human excuse.
4. Laugh lands late and hard: "It was not in the future. It was yesterday, and yesterday has not happened yet according to me." — the funniest words are last, nothing after them.
5. Deadpan throughout: no winking, no stage directions, straight face.

## Final line (194 chars)
> I confirmed the deadline was still in the future by checking my training data, which ends in 2024. It was not in the future. It was yesterday, and yesterday has not happened yet according to me.
